\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys turns dependent reduction fusion into a constrained search for auxiliary states.
A reference representation with a known join specifies the information that the maintained states must retain, so verified recovery determines their join without a separate join-synthesis task.
Source reduction definitions and context corrections guide this search and keep the source quantities available for centred or scaled execution formulas.
The resulting join satisfies the union invariant over the reals; numerical acceptance assesses its emitted floating-point implementation separately.

The recorded TVM derivations cover \nShowFused of \nShowcase operators and reproduce reducers previously derived by hand for softmax, attention and variance.
They demonstrate automatic state augmentation and join derivation for these reduction chains.
Controlled search comparisons and updated numerical measurements will assess the practical effects of the representation choices.
Further work includes scheduling the derived reducers as GPU kernels through TIRx and establishing rigorous floating-point error bounds.
